Privacy & transparency

Privacy Policy

Last reviewed: October 18, 2026

This notice describes the SkyCast website as currently implemented. SkyCast does not require an account. Some operational details depend on the hosting provider and advertising-account configuration; those items are called out below for the site owner to confirm.

Information processed

  • City names entered in search and coordinates when a weather request is made.
  • Precise device coordinates only after you choose the location control and grant your browser's geolocation permission. You can deny or revoke permission in browser settings; a city search remains available.
  • Theme and temperature-unit preferences and saved city names in this browser's local storage. SkyCast's service worker also caches application files for offline shell access. These browser-held values are not associated with a SkyCast account.
  • Standard web request information, such as IP address, time, requested path and user-agent, may be processed by the web host and server for delivery, security and abuse prevention. SkyCast does not define or control the host's log retention period.

Search and location requests pass through SkyCast's server API and are forwarded to the relevant data provider. The browser sends coordinates only when you request a location-based feature or the selected weather data requires coordinates.

Weather and other providers

The current application is configured to use these providers for the stated features:

  • OpenWeather for current conditions.
  • Open-Meteo for geocoding, forecasts, air quality, UV values and (when a paid customer key is configured) weather-model map grids.
  • NOAA/NWS for official US weather alerts; alerts are not available for every location.
  • GNews for live news when its server-side key is configured.
  • NASA GIBS for satellite imagery. Street, terrain and satellite basemaps may load tiles from OpenStreetMap, CARTO and Esri.

External libraries, fonts and static assets may be requested from Google Fonts, jsDelivr, unpkg and cdnjs. These providers receive ordinary network request information. Requests made to external providers are governed by their own privacy notices and terms.

Cookies, advertising and analytics

The application code stores preferences in local storage and does not include an analytics product or an app-authored analytics cookie. Eligible SkyCast pages include the Google AdSense loader once and manually configured ad placements, together with an AdSense publisher declaration and an ads.txt entry. The 404 page does not load ads. Whether Auto ads are enabled is a separate AdSense account setting. Google may use cookies, identifiers or similar technologies to provide, measure or personalize advertising, subject to Google's settings and applicable consent requirements.

Owner action required: confirm whether AdSense Auto ads or other formats are enabled, configure a consent-management platform where required, and update this notice to match the actual account settings and regions served. The current code does not contain an analytics integration.

Retention and security

SkyCast does not provide user accounts or a server-side saved-location profile. Saved locations and display preferences remain in browser storage until cleared by the user or browser. The application server may process transient request data and its hosting platform may retain operational logs.

Owner action required: publish the hosting provider's applicable log-retention period, any server-side cache/log retention details, and a monitored privacy contact. Do not assume that provider or hosting logs are immediately deleted.

Your choices

You may use city search without granting location permission, revoke geolocation access in your browser, and clear SkyCast's local storage/site data to remove saved cities and preferences. For advertising choices, use the controls offered by Google and any consent notice presented on this deployment.

Contact and legal information

For code or service feedback, use the SkyCast project issue tracker. Owner action required: add a monitored privacy contact address and the data controller's legal name and jurisdiction before treating this page as a complete legal disclosure.
