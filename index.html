Lightning heat

Lightning rapidly heats surrounding air, causing expansion that produces thunder.

Source: NOAA National Severe Storms Laboratory
Cloud weight

Clouds contain many water droplets and ice particles, even though their density makes them appear light.

Source: USGS Water Science School
Rain smell

The smell after rain is associated with compounds released by soil, plants and microorganisms.

Source: Royal Meteorological Society

About SkyCast

SkyCast brings weather observations, forecasts, environmental information and Earth data together in one clear experience. Our goal is to make complex atmospheric information easier to understand and more useful in everyday life.

Our Mission
